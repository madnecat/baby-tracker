# Sleep (NextSleepCard, SleepSection) - French review notes

English | French chosen | why unsure

- Sleep has been unsettled the last few days (teething, travel, a growth spurt?). The window is widened to match, and her usual pattern is kept rather than relearned. | Le sommeil est agité depuis quelques jours (poussée dentaire, voyage, pic de croissance ?). La fenêtre est élargie en conséquence, et le rythme habituel est conservé au lieu d'être réappris. | Explains a model behaviour; "pic de croissance" vs "poussée de croissance".
- Already longer than her usual stretch — she could wake any time. | Déjà plus long que d'habitude : le réveil peut arriver à tout moment. | Neutral rewording, drops the pronoun.
- Expected around {time} — she may settle more easily now. | Attendu vers {time}. L'endormissement peut être plus facile maintenant. | Guidance claim; "settle" read as falling asleep.
- Typical at {n} weeks: a-b min awake | Habituel à {n} semaines : a-b min d'éveil | Reference-table wake window; "min d'éveil" is terse.
- Not enough of {name}'s own sleeps ... to predict from her pattern | {name} : pas encore assez de sommeils enregistrés (x/3) pour prévoir à partir du rythme observé. | Name first to avoid "de {name}" elision.
- Sleep still running since / That's longer than {name} usually sleeps ... | Durée supérieure au sommeil habituel pour {name}. ... | Reworded; "Historique" must match the nav label.
- Some sleeps look like they weren't logged ({s} of {n} recent stretches) | Certains sommeils semblent ne pas avoir été enregistrés ({s} sur {n} périodes récentes) ... | "stretches" -> "périodes".
- Wake windows | Périodes d'éveil | Glossary has no entry; "fenêtres d'éveil" is the other common wording (the banner text above uses "fenêtre" for the predicted window).
- Phase 1 shown in two English variants (short on the charts page, long on the card): kept both (sleep.phase.1, sleep.phase.1Long) | Pas encore de rythme jour/nuit / ... : sommeil réparti sur toute la journée | Meaning of "sleeps around the clock".
- Basic "sleep" count: "{n} sleeps" | {n} sommeils | Unusual plural; "périodes de sommeil" could read better.

English keeps the original "she/her" wording (the code only knew the child's name, not sex); only French is neutral.
